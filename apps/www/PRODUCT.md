# AudioBits showcase product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Frontend, game and creative developers discovering AudioBits from a repository,
social link or recommendation. Technically sophisticated designers also explore.

## Product Purpose

Let a visitor hear browser-generated sound, manipulate its character, inspect
portable recipe data and copy a small integration example.

## Positioning

A procedural sound-effects library built on Web Audio, with portable versioned
JSON recipes, typed controls and installed-version metadata.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Eight bundled sounds exercise one-shots, sustained voices, play/live controls and
seeded variation. The homepage session is one lazy Energy Charge engine. The
gallery holds one lazy engine per session. Real caller-owned native analyser
data drives output-reactive detail. No autoplay. Runtime remains independent of
website dependencies.
AudioBits is available on npm. Fumadocs remains a separate reference experience.

## Brand Commitments

Inventive, precise, tactile, playful and confident. Restrained copy. Avoid generic
neon, glass, SaaS gradients, noisy effects and a DAW interface. Sound leads.

## Evidence on Hand

Public exports, recipe metadata, browser signal/lifecycle checks and copyable
examples. Automated evidence does not establish listening quality. The maintainer
reported manual sound-quality acceptance for the current eight sounds on 2026-10-07.
Devices and detailed listening coverage were not specified. Physical device
compatibility is unverified and deliberately deferred.

## Product Principles

- Let one interaction prove the value.
- Express sound intent with portable data and semantic controls.
- Keep sound generation separate from presentation.
- Let real requirements earn new runtime features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and touch operation, labelled controls, visible focus and playback state,
adequate contrast, reduced motion, mute and explicit Stop. No sound-only feedback.

## Route purposes

- `/`: persuade on a first visit. A short explanation sits with one Energy
  Charge instrument. Hold is the proof. The page then distinguishes the
  installable package, eight bundled recipes, and two copy-paste examples, and
  previews Energy Charge, Reactive Thruster, and three bundled sounds without
  starting them. Catalog, documentation, and `npm install audiobits` stay in
  reach. No second live engine.
- `/catalog`: browse every current example and bundled sound. Experiences come first, then sounds. One shared playback session covers the sounds. Experience previews stay silent. The shared header's Catalog item points here. Bundled rows open `/catalog/[slug]`.
- `/catalog/[slug]`: understand and customize one bundled sound. The stage, playback, and inspector stay on one page. Each visit owns one audio session and disposes it on leave.
- `/sounds`: the sound collection and workbenches remain available. The header no longer treats this index as the catalog.
- `/sounds/[slug]`: the earlier workbench remains available until redirects.
- `/experiences/energy-charge`: open the complete charge host and copy that page.
- `/experiences/reactive-thruster`: open the complete movement host and copy that page.
- `/docs`: read the calm Fumadocs reference; marketing components do not enter it.
