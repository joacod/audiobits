# Catalog index

The catalog is the browsing page for every current AudioBits example and bundled sound. It refuses a settings wall, a mixer, and a redirect into one instrument.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ten instruments sit on a dark bench, experiences first and bundled sounds second, with the entry itself as the preview. The page refuses identical cards, a long introduction, and a second audio engine per row.
OWN-WORLD: Live Signal. Void ground, Ink text, Mute labels. Big Shoulders Display names the page and the experiences; Geist Mono runs the index and the controls. The charge preview is a static ring in Ink and Mute. The thruster preview is a still Ink flight field. Bundled rows keep the pulse, harmonic, and flow marks. The signal gradient appears only on live output. Hairline Graphite edges, no cards, no glass, no blobs.
STORY: A visitor recognizes Energy Charge or Reactive Thruster from a static preview and opens its full interactive host. A bundled sound shows its use, plays from one shared session, and opens its existing workbench.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact Catalog heading, a sticky grouped index, and the two experience previews. Bundled sounds follow immediately. On small screens the index becomes a Catalog menu with the same groups.
FORM: Brief-pinned extension of the showcase. No concept-seed roll. Seed key: pinned-by-brief. Code-led.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Review

Verdict: ship. Re-skinned to the Live Signal identity (brand guide v1.0) without changing routes, layout or component boundaries.
