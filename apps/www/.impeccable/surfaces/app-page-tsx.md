# Charge housing: homepage

The homepage persuades by letting a visitor hold Energy Charge, then shows how the package, bundled recipes, and independent examples differ. Fumadocs stays a separate reading surface.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is the charge instrument. The page refuses a second live engine, a specimen-book introduction, and a card grid of claims.
OWN-WORLD: Oxidized mineral-green housing, warm paper labels, brass for charge and primary action, pale signal for hover and focus on the housing. Fraunces carries the voice and the charge numeral. Arial runs the controls. Monospace is only for code. Concentric rings are the mark. Hairline rules, no cards, no glass, no neon.
STORY: A visitor understands that sound is generated in the browser, holds one voice, then sees the package, eight bundled recipes, and two copy-paste examples. Catalog, docs, and the install command stay in reach.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header and hero share one green field. The headline and a short explanation sit beside the charge well on wide screens and directly above it on small screens. Hold to charge is the primary action. Explore the catalog and Start building are the text actions.
FORM: Brief-pinned extension of the Step 1 Energy Charge housing. No concept-seed roll. Seed key: pinned-by-brief. Code-led.
FINISH: Reviewed in-thread after the shipped finish-reviewer type was unavailable. The homepage, shared header, and token record in DESIGN.md describe the charge housing that shipped.
